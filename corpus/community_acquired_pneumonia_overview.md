## Excerpt

Pneumonia by definition is an infection of the lung or pulmonary parenchyma.

CAP itself is a common infection and is often misdiagnosed and inappropriately treated.
While it can often present as a relatively mild illness, overall it still remains a
cause of considerable morbidity and mortality.

Although there are a large number of microbial pathogens that have been associated with
CAP, in the immunocompetent patient, the list is relatively short and reasonably stable.
The most common causes include the pneumococcus, atypicals, such as Mycoplasma
pneumoniae and Chlamydophila pneumoniae, Staphylococcus aureus and certain Gram-negative
rods.
